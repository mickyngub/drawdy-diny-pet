# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- republished build, no changes since 2.0.0

## 2.0.0

- a new look: Diny is now Drawdy's mint Viking dino, holding a lime marker, with every pose from the mascot bible
- at rest it looks happy, thinks with the marker at its chin, sketches or chats; when no shape is in reach it shrugs
- set it down after carrying it and it wags its tail
- click it again while it shouts and it cheers; a third quick click brings out its toy sword for a battle cry, a slash and a salute
- after ten hops it gloves up for a Muay Thai workout: a wai kru, then a jab, cross, kick and push kick, and a victory pose
- it stands with a soft shadow under its feet, and its mouth hangs wide only when it shouts
- the rail button and the listing icon show the new Diny

## 1.1.0

- Diny stands on what you drew, not the box around it: along a pencil stroke or a line, over a circle or a diamond, on the upper edge of a rotated shape
- it keeps to the board: never under the sidebar or past the board's edges, even while you carry it
- it hops onto the minimap too, rides it down when you fold the minimap, and drops off when you hide it
- grab Diny and drag it anywhere on the board; it dangles while held and drops onto whatever is below when you let go
- after a good run of hopping it gets tired and naps for half a minute; click it to wake it up
- new faces: it waves hello where it first lands, jumps and falls with their own faces, and rests with its mouth open a little or hanging wide

## 1.0.0

First public release

- a Diny button on the board: turn Diny on and it drops into your view, turn it off and it leaves at once
- Diny hops between the tops of the shapes in view and the bottom of the screen, a short hop at a time, facing the way it goes
- it stands only on flat shapes at least about 60 px wide on screen, never on frames or rotated shapes
- it rides along on its shape when you pan or zoom, and stays the same size on screen
- it drops straight down when its shape is deleted, and drops back in from the top when its spot leaves the view or you open another board
- click Diny and it shouts with a little pop; a click mid-hop shouts on landing
- only you see it: Diny is an overlay on your screen, nothing on the board changes, and only its own box takes clicks
- Diny stays on, board after board, until you turn it off; that choice is the only thing saved, in this browser
- without the board permission Diny keeps to the bottom of the screen
