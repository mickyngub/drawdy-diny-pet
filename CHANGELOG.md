# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- A Diny button on the board: press it and Diny drops into your view
- Diny hops between the tops of the shapes in view and along the bottom of the screen, facing the way it goes
- Click Diny and it shouts
- Diny rides along on its shape when you pan or zoom, and drops back in from the top when its shape leaves the view or is deleted
- Only you see it: Diny is an overlay on your screen, nothing on the board changes, and it takes clicks only on its own box
- Diny stays on, board after board, until you press the button again
